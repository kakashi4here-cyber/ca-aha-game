FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ChartedGyan, and what is it for?
ChartedGyan is a learning and practice platform designed for CA students. It helps students revise concepts, test their knowledge through interactive quizzes, and understand topics through instant explanations. Our aim is to make learning engaging, accessible, and consistent.
Why should I choose ChartedGyan as my learning companion?
ChartedGyan boosts your learning and revision by transforming theory-heavy courses like CA, CS, and CMA into fun, interactive, and engaging experiences. It makes concepts easier to revise, improves retention, and turns learning into something you actually look forward to.
Where did the idea of ChartedGyan come from?
ChartedGyan was born from the idea of bridging the gap between the struggles of traditional learning and effortless performance. We aim to make learning simpler, revision easier, and preparation more engaging.
What will I get at ChartedGyan?

At ChartedGyan, you’ll get an interactive learning and practice experience built around your CA preparation. From chapter-wise and topic-specific questions to Daily Case Studies, Past Year Papers, MTPs and RTPs, you’ll have multiple ways to strengthen your preparation.

You can also explore concepts through practical explanations, conceptual games, flashcards, keywords and formulas. With features like Keyword of the Day, daily challenges, leaderboards, activity tracking, doubts, SWOT analysis and free resources, ChartedGyan aims to make your preparation more engaging, consistent and effective.

Is ChartedGyan only for revision?

No. ChartedGyan is for every kind of CA student, whether you are a complete beginner or have just 1.5 days left before the exam. Beginners use it to build the habit of practice from day one, and last-minute students use it to revise everything quickly and fix weak spots.

Why should I go through my syllabus again on ChartedGyan?

You do not have to read it again. Syllabus Completion simply lets you tick off chapters and units from the ICAI Study Material, so you can see what is done, what is left and where to practise next. It is your checklist, not a new course.

Does ChartedGyan teach the subjects?

No, and it is not trying to. Your classes and ICAI Study Material teach. ChartedGyan makes you strong through the Practice section, where you test yourself until a topic is solid, and the Concept section, where you can revise a subject in one go with flashcards, keywords, formulas and calculator tricks.

What is Gyan XP?

Gyan XP is ChartedGyan’s learning currency. You earn XP for study habits that matter, such as finishing the Daily Case Study, completing topic tests, scoring 80% or more, attempting past papers, hitting weekly targets and keeping a 7-day streak.

Your XP collects in your learning wallet and counts towards the leaderboard. The values shown on the site are illustrative, and the final earning rules will be announced soon.
